🕊️ Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This page sets out the child safety standards for the Whispr mobile application ("Whispr" or the "App"), operated by Greenvision Labs ("we", "us", "our"). It applies to everyone who uses Whispr and forms part of our Terms of Service (see section 14, Child Safety).

1. Zero Tolerance for Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation

Whispr and Greenvision Labs have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). Any content, behaviour, or use of Whispr that sexualises, grooms, exploits, endangers, or otherwise harms a minor is strictly prohibited. This includes child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form, whether shared in chats, profile photos, gallery photos, or anywhere else in the App.

This prohibition applies without exception. Accounts that violate it are permanently banned, and we report such cases to the relevant authorities as described below.

2. Whispr Is for Adults Only (18+)

You must be at least 18 years old to use Whispr. Users confirm their date of birth when creating an account, and any account we find or reasonably believe to belong to a person under 18 will be suspended and removed.

3. How to Report a Concern

You can report a child safety concern without leaving the App.

  • Report User: choose Report User from any of these places, then select the "Child safety concern" reason:
    • the user's profile (⋮ menu)
    • the menu inside a chat
    • a long-press on the conversation in your Chats list
    • a long-press on a specific message
    You can add details and attach screenshots to help us review the report. A specific profile or gallery photo can also be reported using Report this photo while viewing it.
  • Contact Whispr: go to Settings → Report a Problem (or Send Feedback) and select "Child safety concern" to send a message directly to our team from inside the App.
  • Email: write to our Child Safety Point of Contact at privacy@getwhisprd.com.

You can also block any user at any time so they can no longer contact you.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police or emergency services first. You can also report child sexual abuse material directly to:

  • the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline: report.cybertip.org
  • the Internet Watch Foundation (IWF): report.iwf.org.uk
  • in India, the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal: cybercrime.gov.in

4. How We Act on Reports

  • Review: every report is reviewed by our team. Child safety reports are prioritised above all other reports.
  • Removal and bans: accounts involved in CSAE, or belonging to a minor, are suspended and permanently banned, and the offending content is removed. We may also block the device associated with a banned account from creating new accounts.
  • Preservation of evidence: we preserve the report, any attached screenshots or reported photos, and the relevant account information, so that it can be provided to the authorities as required by law. Report and moderation records are retained even after an account is deleted.
  • Reporting to authorities: we report apparent CSAM and child sexual exploitation to the relevant authorities as required by applicable law. This includes the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), the Internet Watch Foundation (IWF), and law enforcement agencies in the relevant jurisdiction, including India.
  • Cooperation: we respond to valid requests from law enforcement relating to child safety investigations.

Messages on Whispr are end-to-end encrypted, so we cannot read your conversations. The details and screenshots you include in a report are what allow us to review and act on it quickly.

5. Prevention

  • Whispr is restricted to users aged 18 and over, and accounts found to belong to minors are removed.
  • Users who are reported repeatedly receive escalating restrictions, up to suspension and a permanent ban.
  • Gallery photos are private and only unlocked between two users who have both chosen to connect.
