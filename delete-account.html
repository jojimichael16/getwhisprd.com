🕊️ Delete Your Whispr Account

You can request deletion of your Whispr account and associated data from here, without needing the app installed.

How it works

  1. Submit the request form below using the email address associated with your Whispr account.
  2. We verify the request is genuinely yours and initiate deletion, matching the same process used when deleting your account from within the app.
  3. Your account enters a 30-day grace period. During this time, if you log back into the app, you'll be offered the option to restore your account.
  4. If you do not log back in within 30 days, your account and data are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.
This action cannot be undone after the 30-day grace period. If you're unsure, you can also request deletion directly from within the app via Profile → Settings → Delete Account, which follows the identical process.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, including username, bio, interests, and profile photo
  • Your gallery photos
  • Your conversations and messages
  • Your Whispr connections
  • Your presence and account activity data

What we retain, and why

Consistent with our Privacy Policy, we retain a limited record of any reports or moderation actions involving your account for up to 12 months after deletion, even once your account itself is removed. This is necessary to maintain the safety of our community and to meet our obligations under our Terms of Service and applicable child safety laws. This retained record does not include your profile, photos, or message content.

Request deletion

Verification: To protect your account from unauthorized deletion requests, we check every request against our records before taking any action. We will only proceed once we've confirmed the request genuinely comes from the email address associated with the account — for example, by replying to you at that address for confirmation. We will never delete an account based solely on someone else providing your email or username.

Fill in the details below. This will open your email app with a pre-filled deletion request addressed to our support team.
